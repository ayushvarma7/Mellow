Iconography

Drawn faces, not emoji

Five hand-built vector faces replace the emoji the app started with. They render identically on every device and carry their own colour, so no tinted backdrop is needed.

Surfaces & accents

Mascot

Meet Sprout

A calm meditating seedling, built as an Android VectorDrawable — no external art, no Lottie. Sprout appears in the breathing orb, the journal prompt, and empty states.

Sprout is deliberately simple geometry — two leaves, a honey gradient body, closed eyes and a soft smile — so it scales cleanly from a 44dp orb badge to a 128dp empty-state hero without ever rasterising.
